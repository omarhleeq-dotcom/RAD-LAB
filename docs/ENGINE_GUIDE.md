# Engine and content constraints

Read only the sections relevant to the change, as routed by [the docs index](README.md). Session workflow is maintained only in [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md); task state is [project-status](project-status.md). These are existing constraints relocated from CLAUDE, not new medical/implementation decisions.

## Architecture and optional mechanisms

- **Exams/cases are data:** `ExamDefinition` in `src/data/exams/*` holds controls, rules, tolerances, weights/critical findings, protocol and scene/render bindings; `CaseDefinition` in `src/data/cases/*` holds patient/order, baseline, cooperation, attenuation and initial state. `composeCase()` yields the `RadCase` compatibility view. Validate with `validateExam` / `validateCase`; no case-specific UI logic.
- **Separation:** `domain/` ← pure `features/evaluation|scoring` (no React) ← stores ← UI. Simulation/scoring never lives in React or pixels. Technique selectable controls, target, tolerance and evidence class are distinct data. Prefer reusable components; avoid unnecessary dependencies/global state.
- Scene registry (`scenes.tsx`), `SceneLayout` / `DetectorFigure` and projection registry (`src/data/projections.ts`) supply art, second view, optional cues, texts and wording. `SceneId`, `BodyPartId`, `ProjectionId`, `WordingNs`, effect/critical/variant ids are extensible and validated against their registries; no projection flags in shared positioning components. New anatomy/effects must fit existing mechanisms or have a separate reference-led generalisation.
- Optional fields mean **unused when omitted**: no hidden controls, fallback scoring or feedback (S-STD-1). Chest cues, breathing, inspiration response, lung landmarks and AEC are optional; manual-only exams omit AEC. Existing documented data fallbacks are not permission to introduce new ones. Available mechanisms and precise touch-points: [recipe §2](exam-recipe.md#2-s11-generic-mechanisms-what-an-exam-may-use).
- Learner chooses the instruction, never the defect; inspiration/motion are deterministic `patientCooperation` data, independent of each other and technique. Keep-still is weight0 until an educator defines its rubric. No grid control or patient-build mechanism is implied by contextual text (S11-3/4).

## Medical evidence, scoring and rendering

- **Never invent medical facts or diagnostic radiographs.** Clinical values remain `provisional: true` + TODO pending educator/physicist review O5. Every medical rule has one of the five `EVIDENCE_CLASSES`; classify each value SOURCE-BACKED / TEACHING CHOICE / OWNER DECISION. Teaching choices stay labelled in [REVIEW_PACKAGE](REVIEW_PACKAGE.md) and the docs; in the UI they follow the [UI inventory decision](DECISIONS.md#ui-inventory-owner-2026-10-22) (no "provisional/unreviewed" wording, no validation claim); unvalidated imagery remains a labelled placeholder.
- **Reference first (S10-2):** before generalisation or implementation, the exam/modality reference must be merged to main with complete Needs (same/differs/new/unknown; each unknown names the missing source). References run ahead of code; generalise only for concrete merged needs, never placeholders. Use [reference template](templates/EXAM_REFERENCE_TEMPLATE.md) and [exam recipe](exam-recipe.md); no new exams while the freeze holds.
- **BONTRAGER-9 governs X-ray positioning/technique** (printed pages, S10b-25). Other sources support; on conflict Bontrager wins; where silent use the next applicable official source (EC16260, ACR-CHEST/ACR-PORTABLE, WHO-TECH, WHO-CHEST), then owner decision. This does not clinically validate provisional values. Read [source notes/register](sources/REGISTER.md), not uncommitted PDFs; advisor-read claims are not independently re-verified by an agent. Abstracts alone cannot establish a rule/value/convention; PMC full text only via official NCBI E-utilities; no captcha bypass, unofficial mirrors or caches.
- Facts are paraphrased/cited, short quotes only; no textbook text/figures or third-party student notes. **Commercial compatibility (S10-3):** own work, PD/CC0, CC BY or purchased licence; NC/ND excluded, SA only with explicit owner approval. Radiopaedia assets remain excluded; only the owner-supplied SID fact citations are permitted by the narrow [RAD-081 exception](DECISIONS.md#rad081-owner-2026-10-25), without copied content or independent-verification claims. Every external asset is registered in [provenance](ASSET_PROVENANCE_AND_LICENSES.md) in the same commit; PDFs/secrets are never committed. Image sourcing/approval and documented exceptions are in recipe §5 and the asset register; never turn owner verification into independent verification or reopen an explicitly settled licence audit automatically.
- Scoring/category/check weights are exam configuration, categories sum to100. Collimation belongs to `positioning`. **Marker is per exam:** `markerRule`, placement and critical list are distinct from numeric weight; Chest PA `side-marker` stays weight0 while missing/wrong markers are critical gates (S11b-1). Other exams may have weighted checks. `fixed-side` judges the letter without a viewer-half rule; its side is exam data, not a duplicate case field (O29). `markerKeepOut` is optional, with the documented whole-required-anatomy exclusion when absent. Never impose one universal marker/scoring policy.
- Renderer consumes `EvaluationResult.resultState`: `RadiographResultState` → `buildRenderPlan` → pure `renderRadiograph`; canvas only draws. No pixel read-back into state/scoring, no unseeded randomness. Magnitudes live in `features/radiograph/tuning.ts` and are educational approximations. `ImageVariantId` comes from failed/partial checks; `ResultImage` swaps validated imagery. Low exposure means noise/detail loss, never brightness; excess exposure never darkens; digital brightness is not an exposure indicator. No invented EI/EIT, universal DI threshold or clinical numeric mAs target (a labelled model anchor is different).
- Rotation sign is defined by the **result image**, per exam (S11c-1/S13-6); the old universal physical-turn wording does not hold for AP. Preserve the open plan-view mismatch O19; new projections need a recorded sign and renderer test. Magnitude-only feedback where sources give no direction. Anatomy/markers and SVG/canvas stay LTR and never mirror in RTL.

## Scene design and bilingual UI

- Reuse `scene-style.ts` tokens (`SCENE`, `STROKE`, `UNDER`, `LABEL_SIZE`), `SceneParts.tsx` and existing figures. Never invent hex/stroke values. Dark muted anatomy, thin bone/edge lines (chest figures: [chest drawing style](#chest-drawing-style); hand: [limb drawing style](#limb-drawing-style)); ONE amber accent over dark understroke; guides at most green target + sky reference/fault, also distinct by shape/text. Accents ≥3:1 against every fill (`scene-style.test.ts`); labels ≥7:1 on measured dark pills, no stroke halos on Arabic joins.
- Draw scored faults visibly by shape, with outlines/labels only when guides ON. If an honest cue cannot be drawn, label/record that limit and apply the honesty gate before implementation. Clip to detector/viewBox, no dead space or unexplained shapes; each drawing has settings-derived text alternative. Keep pointer/keyboard/ARIA semantics. Use family-appropriate axis/hint labels, not universal Head/Feet. Measure overlaps at390/1440 EN/AR, guides ON/OFF and all moving axes ([recipe §4a](exam-recipe.md#4a-label-collision-measurement-reusable-script-s14-0)).
- UI strings in both `messages/en.json` and `messages/ar.json`, synced keys; data text `LocalizedText`; logical CSS (`ms-`, `ps-`, `border-s-`), numbers/units `dir="ltr"`. New Arabic terms remain machine-assisted DRAFT in [ARABIC_TERMINOLOGY](ARABIC_TERMINOLOGY.md) until reviewed; use «الكاشف». Keep assessment answer separation.

<a id="chest-drawing-style"></a>
### Chest drawing style (RAD-072 1b)

Applies to every chest figure (PA/AP frontal, lateral, plan view, AP side view); other families keep the rule above. Owner instruction: [RAD-072 1b](DECISIONS.md#rad072-1b-owner-2026-10-23).

1. **Proportions:** one set, `BODY` in `scene-style.ts`, in drawing cm of one schematic adult (head, neck, shoulder width, thorax width/depth, trunk, arm and leg lengths/thicknesses), multiplied by each view's own scale. **Drawing proportions, not medical facts or measurements.** The thorax follows the existing fitted landmark boxes plus soft-tissue margin; the boxes never move to fit a drawing.
2. **Tonal layers, back to front:** detector background → soft tissue (`soft`, mid tone) → lung fields (`air`, darkest) → heart/mediastinum (`heart`, filled) → bones (`boneFill`/`boneLine`, lighter, stronger stroke) → guides (accents, dashed, thinner than bones, on under-stroke) → fault overlays (outline/hatch, translucent, never a solid dark fill) → labels.
3. **Contrast (WCAG 1.4.11 / 1.4.3):** adjacent filled regions ≥3:1; where one luminance axis cannot also keep bones ≥3:1 (heart beside soft tissue) the shared outline is ≥3:1 to both. Meaningful strokes ≥3:1 to the fills they cross; accents ≥3:1 directly or via their under-stroke; label text ≥4.5:1 on its pill over any chest fill. The scene panel is the fixed dark console in both UI themes, so the same tokens hold in dark and light; `scene-style.test.ts` pins every pair.
4. **Labels** stay fully inside the drawing frame at 390 and 1440, EN and AR, guides ON/OFF; chest guide labels are drawn above the collimation shade.

<a id="limb-drawing-style"></a>
### Limb drawing style (RAD-072 2a hand pilot, 2b wrist and foot)

Applies to limb figures drawn from traced outlines; today the hand PA / lateral, wrist PA / lateral, foot DP / oblique and shoulder Grashey detector views. Other limb figures (knee, ankle, elbow, pelvis) keep the rule above until their own owner decision. Owner instructions: [RAD-072 2a](DECISIONS.md#rad072-2a-hand-owner-2026-10-23), [2b](DECISIONS.md#rad072-2b-owner-2026-10-23), [2c](DECISIONS.md#rad072-2c-owner-2026-10-24).

1. **Source:** outlines traced from the project's own baselines only (`scripts/trace-outlines/`, `hand-outlines.json`): illustrative, drawing-only, not a measurement. They are moved by the family's existing model (`features/hand-model`, unchanged), each structure following the model part it names, so the drawing turns like the result image. A right-hand case mirrors the drawing; RTL never does.
2. **Tonal layers, back to front:** detector background → soft tissue (`soft`, `outline` edge) → joint spaces (`air`, thin) → bone fills (`boneFill`, **translucent**, `LIMB.boneFillOpacity`; the copies of a superimposed group share it so they read as one bone at the base pose) → every bone's cortex edge last (`boneLine` on `underStroke`) → guides → fault overlays (outline, never a solid fill) → labels. Tokens: the chest `SCENE` tones plus the `LIMB` widths in `scene-style.ts`; no new colour.
3. **Superimposed bones stay visible** (radiograph-style): overlapping fills read lighter and every edge is drawn over all fills, so no bone disappears at any rotation (`hand-outline-drawing.test.ts`, `wrist-outline-drawing.test.ts`, `foot-outline-drawing.test.ts`, full range, every exam). The layers live in `outline-layers.tsx` and the shared geometry in `outline-geometry.ts`; each family has its own pure projection (`hand-`, `wrist-`, `foot-outline-drawing.ts`). Contrast as in the chest rule 3: edges ≥3:1 on soft tissue, one bone and two bones via the under-stroke; pinned by `scene-style.test.ts`. Guides stay thinner than a bone edge.
4. Structures marked `drawn: false` in the data (the sesamoid-like densities) and single-point landmarks are not drawn.
5. **Wrist (2b):** PA = the model's column anchors warp the whole image, the ulna and the radius move as two layers below the radiocarpal rows; lateral = two copies (far 40 % / near 60 %) of the two superimposed bands. Bones cut by the image edge are open outlines whose fill follows the image border.
6. **Foot (2b):** every bone moves as a RIGID piece by the model's displacement at that bone (owner finding #13: the result image's band warp shears bones; the drawing does not reproduce the shear; the shear itself is a model limit, not changed here). The tarsals are not displaced by the model and stay put.
7. **Side views (2b):** proportions only, read from the traced outlines (`wristSideHeights`, `footSideProportions`); no geometry or scoring change.
