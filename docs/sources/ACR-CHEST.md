## General / scope
- **Sec I, IV:** the routine examination is an erect **PA plus a LEFT lateral** at full inspiration; it may be modified by the physician or a qualified technologist. Adults who cannot stand, or who are at risk of falling, may have a sitting **AP** instead of the PA.

## Chest PA (and AP — "PA/AP" in the parameter)
- **Sec IV (examination specifications):** the PA/AP image includes both apices and the costophrenic sulci; the scapulae and arms should not obscure the lungs; the vertebral column is centred between the clavicles; the lower thoracic vertebral bodies and the retrocardiac vessels are defined; collimation is limited to the listed structures and does not go beyond the receptor.
- **Sec IV (technical, adults):** exposure time for the PA not over 40 ms; high-kV technique 120–150 kVp; anti-scatter at least equal to a 10:1 grid, preferably 12:1 (grid or air gap); technique charts posted; lung parenchyma displayed at mid-grey.
- **Sec VI (equipment):** grid for adults (at least 10:1, preferably 12:1); rectangular collimation; **SID at least 72 in (183 cm — conversion, not source) for routine upright**, **40 in (102 cm — conversion, not source) when clinically necessary** (e.g. supine, immobilised patient); focal spot maximum 2.0 mm, 0.6–1.2 mm recommended.

## Chest LATERAL
- **Sec I, IV:** the lateral in the routine examination is the **LEFT** lateral (see General). The parameter's lateral-specific image criteria were not part of the notes supplied.

## Chest AP
- **Sec I, IV:** sitting AP may replace the PA for adults unable to stand or at fall risk. AP technique for portable work is in [ACR-PORTABLE](ACR-PORTABLE.md).

## Quality control / identification
- **Sec IV (QC):** each image is permanently marked, including the **right or left side and the patient position** (plus facility, patient identification, date and time). Presence of the marker is stated; **no viewer-side display convention** was part of the notes supplied.

## How RAD-LAB may use this source
- `common-reference` (US practice parameter) for: left lateral as the routine lateral, kV 120–150, grid ≥ 10:1, SID ≥ 183 cm upright / ≥ 102 cm when necessary, mid-grey display, marker presence incl. patient position.
- Not for a marker letter / viewer-side convention, a numeric scoring tolerance, or a mAs target.
