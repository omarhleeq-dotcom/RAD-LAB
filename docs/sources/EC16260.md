## General
- **PDF p.15** — The criteria apply to standard-size adult patients (70 kg). Each projection has three parts: image criteria, a dose criterion, and an example technique. An image that meets all the clinical requirements must never be rejected only because some of the image criteria are not met.

## Chest PA
- **PDF p.20 — image criteria:**
  - full inspiration, judged by the ribs above the diaphragm (6 anterior or 10 posterior), with suspended respiration;
  - symmetric reproduction, shown by the spinous process lying centrally between the medial ends of the clavicles;
  - medial borders of the scapulae outside the lung fields;
  - the whole rib cage above the diaphragm visible;
  - sharp reproduction of the vascular pattern out to the periphery;
  - sharp trachea and proximal bronchi, heart and aortic borders, diaphragm and lateral costophrenic angles;
  - retrocardiac lung and mediastinum visible; spine visible through the heart.
- **PDF p.20 — dose criterion:** entrance surface dose 0.3 mGy (standard patient).
- **PDF p.20 — example technique:** vertical stand with grid (ratio 10, 40 lines/cm); focal spot ≤ 1.3; total filtration ≥ 3.0 mm Al; FFD 180 (140–200) cm; 125 kV; AEC with the **right lateral chamber**; exposure time < 20 ms.

## Chest LATERAL ("if indicated after viewing the PA")
- **PDF p.21 — image criteria:**
  - full inspiration with suspended respiration;
  - arms raised clear of the thorax;
  - posterior lung borders superimposed;
  - trachea shown; costophrenic angles shown;
  - sharp posterior heart border, aorta, mediastinum, diaphragm, sternum and thoracic spine;
  - small-detail sizes are the same as for the PA.
- **PDF p.21 — dose criterion:** entrance surface dose 1.5 mGy, i.e. 5 times the PA value (1.5 / 0.3).
- **PDF p.21 — example technique:** same device, grid, focal spot, filtration and FFD as the PA; 125 kV; AEC with the **central chamber**; exposure time < 40 ms.
