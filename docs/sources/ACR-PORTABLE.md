## General / scope
- **Sec I, II:** applies to critically ill or unstable patients and those who cannot be transported; device checks are part of the practice.

## Chest AP (portable / mobile)
- **Sec IV (geometry):**
  - cooperative adults, fully upright portable: **SID 40–72 in (102–183 cm — conversion, not source), as close as possible to 72 in**;
  - unresponsive or uncooperative patients: supine or semi-erect, **SID 40 in (102 cm — conversion, not source) or more**;
  - keep the patient-to-receptor distance as small as possible.
- **Sec IV (image):** peak inspiration for most indications; include the apices, costophrenic sulci, upper airway and upper abdomen; optimal penetration shows the retrocardiac vessels and the lower thoracic spine.
- **Sec IV (technical, adults):** without a grid 70–100 kVp; with a grid, more than 100 kVp may be used; lung parenchyma displayed at mid-grey; exposure time as short as feasible; **record mAs, kVp, distance and patient position for each image**.
- **Sec IV (QC):** the accuracy of laterality labelling should be checked.
- **Sec VI (equipment):** the unit must reach acceptable exposure times (100 ms for adults, 30 ms for newborns / infants).
