## General (Projections)
- In AP the posterior structures are nearer the detector; in PA the anterior structures are.
- A lateral projection means the central ray passes from one side of the patient to the other.

## Chest PA
- Erect; chin slightly extended; hands on hips with palms out; shoulders rolled forward; central ray at the centre of the lung fields; no rotation.
- PA minimises cardiac magnification.

## Chest LATERAL
- **LEFT** side against the receptor (the stated reason: it minimises cardiac magnification).
- Arms raised and crossed above the head; chin extended; no rotation or tilt.
- Thorax centred on the central ray and on the receptor in the anterior–posterior direction.
- (Lateral *decubitus*, a different projection: patient on either side, arms above the head, chin up, central ray at T7. Not a lateral-erect rule; do not transfer it.)

## Chest AP
- Portable chest radiographs are AP by necessity and magnify the heart.
- No AP positioning detail (arms, shoulders, SID, tube angulation) was found in the notes.
