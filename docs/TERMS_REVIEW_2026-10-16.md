## Reading key and stop/report boundary

MATCH = sentence agrees with the reviewed implementation or is an instruction/intention clearly identified as such. TOO NARROW = materially incomplete inventory/purpose. TOO BROAD = absolute or wider guarantee than evidence supports. UNVERIFIED = owner/provider/operational fact cannot be established from repo. Mixed sentences receive the material limiting classification; specific uncertainties stay explicit. A schema permission is not proof a client emits it or that the live deployment exactly matches Git.
